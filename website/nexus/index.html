NEXUS

A lightweight, private communication client built natively for Android. Focused entirely on clean 1:1 messaging and topic-based channels without social media feeds, stories, or surveillance telemetry.

Illustrative preview

What it does

NEXUS strips messaging down to its essential utility: prompt, unencumbered communication with people and topics you deliberately choose to follow.

Instead of algorithmic attention loops, NEXUS prioritizes quiet utility: fast text transmission, clean image sharing, and instant local search across your historical chat archive.

How it works

Conversations are cached in an encrypted SQLite database on your device. Network exchanges utilize low-overhead binary WebSockets to minimize battery and memory consumption on mobile hardware.

NEXUS incorporates zero third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or cross-service profiling beacons.

Privacy & Development Status

NEXUS is currently in active development at our Pune studio. Before public release, comprehensive architectural documentation and cryptographic specifications will be published.
