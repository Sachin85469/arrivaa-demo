NEXUS

In development

A private messaging app built around direct chats and topic channels.

In development · Source coming soon Follow on GitHub

What it does

NEXUS is a messaging client designed to stay out of your way. Modern messaging apps are routinely cluttered with social stories, engagement algorithms, and surveillance telemetry that drain attention and battery. NEXUS strips all of that away.

The app focuses strictly on two modes of conversation: direct one-to-one chats and topic-oriented group channels. There are no algorithmic feeds, no disappearing stories, and no ad trackers embedded in the code.

All message records reside locally in a sandboxed SQLite database on your device. You retain full control over your conversation history with simple self-serve data clearing.

How it works

NEXUS is being built natively for Android using Kotlin. It prioritises minimal background resource consumption, targeting under 2% battery drain over a full 24-hour cycle by avoiding persistent analytics polling and unnecessary network wake locks.

Local data storage is powered by Android Room SQLite, keeping your personal conversation threads contained entirely within the application sandbox.

Under the hood

NEXUS is built with a focus on native Android performance and clean architecture:

  • Language & Runtime: Kotlin on modern Android Runtime (ART).
  • Storage: On-device SQLite via Room DB.
  • Telemetry: 0 tracking SDKs, 0 third-party advertising libraries.
  • Availability: In active development. Source code will be published on GitHub under the MIT licence when the initial test build is ready.

Privacy and your data

NEXUS stores message history locally on your device. When NEXUS launches, it will have its own dedicated privacy policy. In the meantime, you can review our studio privacy standards.
