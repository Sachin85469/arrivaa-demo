What it is

NEXUS is an early-stage project exploring lightweight, privacy-centered messaging. Modern communication platforms have evolved into engagement-driven networks dominated by algorithmic feeds, status updates, and advertising beacons. NEXUS returns to pure messaging utility.

Conversations are organized into direct chats and focused topic spaces. Message logs are stored in a local SQLite database on your device sandbox, avoiding centralized message mining.

Architectural commitments

  • Local data ownership: Messages and cached media are stored in device storage, purgeable with a single tap.
  • No algorithmic ranking: Messages appear strictly in chronological order without engagement filtering or suggestions.
  • Zero tracking SDKs: No third-party behavioral analytics or advertising beacons embedded in the codebase.
  • Open development: When test builds are ready, source code will be published on GitHub under open-source licensing.

Privacy disclosure

NEXUS is currently in active development and has not yet been released for public testing. Read our preliminary architectural privacy notice for further details.
