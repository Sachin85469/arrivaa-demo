Privacy Notice

ARRIVA NEXUS · Android Application

Effective date: November 3, 2026

In development

NEXUS is currently in software development and has not been published to app marketplaces or open beta distribution. This document outlines the architectural commitments that will govern the application when released.

Architectural Privacy Commitments

  • Local-first storage: Direct conversations and topic channels will be persisted in an on-device SQLite database within the app's protected sandbox.
  • No advertising trackers: The codebase will contain zero third-party advertising SDKs, demographic profiling beacons, or behavioral tracking libraries.
  • Chronological order: Message streams will follow strict chronological sequencing without engagement ranking algorithms.
  • Full data deletion: Users will have complete control to wipe conversation histories and purge local caches directly within the app interface.

Full Policy Publication

A comprehensive, production-grade privacy policy detailing cryptographic implementations, network protocols, and data safety points will be published on this page before the first public test build is released.
