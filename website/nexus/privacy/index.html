NEXUS Privacy Policy

ARRIVA NEXUS — Android Messaging Application

Effective date: November 3, 2026

In development

NEXUS is currently in active software development and has not yet been released to the public or submitted to app marketplaces.

This page serves as an initial privacy statement outlining our core architectural commitments. A comprehensive, production-grade privacy policy detailing all runtime permissions, cryptographic specifications, and data safety points will be published here prior to the release of the first public test build.

Architectural Privacy Principles

When NEXUS is released, its architecture will adhere to the following principles:

  • On-device SQLite storage: Direct messages and channel discussions will be stored in an SQLite database on your device sandbox.
  • Zero advertising trackers: The codebase will not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral telemetry trackers, or demographic profiling beacons.
  • No social algorithms: Messages will appear in simple chronological order with no algorithmic ranking, recommendations, or engagement loops.
  • Local data erasure: Users will be able to delete conversation threads and purge all local message stores at any time directly through the app interface.
