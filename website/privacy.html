Privacy Policy for Arriva Travel Companion

This document explains the technical justification, collection boundaries, local sandboxing, and data deletion procedures for the Arriva Android application (package: com.arriva.touristguide).

Commercial Data Sale 0% (Strictly Prohibited)
Location Tracking Foreground Only
Sensitive Permissions 0 SMS / Call Declarations
Data Purge Window ≤ 7 Business Days

01. Scope & Core Architectural Guarantee

Arriva is engineered on an Offline-First, Privacy-by-Design paradigm. The application operates primarily against a local Room SQLite database on your Android hardware. We do not maintain advertising identifiers, behavioral profile brokers, or biometric trackers. We categorically never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose user data to data brokers.

02. Android Runtime Permissions & Technical Justifications

In accordance with Google Play's Prominent Disclosure and Consent policies, below are the specific permissions requested by Arriva:

ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Runtime Permission

Technical Justification: Required to position your marker on topographic hiking trails (osmdroid) and urban transit maps (Google Maps SDK). Coordinates are also used to compute distance vectors in the Simulated Annealing trip planner and to pre-fill coordinates into your SMS composer during an emergency distress action.
Boundary: Processed in memory while the app is active in the foreground. Never logged to external analytics servers or transmitted without user action. Arriva declares NO background location permission (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION).

CAMERA & READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Optional) Runtime Permission

Technical Justification: Requested only when you intentionally upload a photographic review of a heritage spot or culinary destination.
Boundary: The camera and gallery picker are only invoked on explicit user touch. Arriva never scans your photo library in the background.

INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Install-Time Permission

Technical Justification: Used to download offline map tile packages during setup, authenticate optional user accounts via Supabase, and dispatch prompts to our secure backend proxy.
Boundary: Encrypted using TLS 1.3 across all network transactions.

EXPLICIT EXCLUSION: SEND_SMS & CALL_PHONE Zero Dangerous Permissions

To guarantee complete Play Store compliance, Arriva does NOT declare SEND_SMS, CALL_PHONE, or BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE. All emergency actions utilize standard Android system Intents (Intent.ACTION_SENDTO and Intent.ACTION_DIAL). The user retains complete control before any message is sent or call is placed.

03. Third-Party Infrastructure & Data Telemetry

Arriva connects only to vetted infrastructure components required for core utility:

  • Supabase: Handles optional user authentication and public community reviews. Data stored in enterprise PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security (RLS).
  • Render Backend Proxy: Intermediates Claude AI requests and image uploads to prevent client-side token exposure. User IP addresses are not logged to disk.
  • Cloudinary: Stores user-submitted review imagery with optimized compression.
  • Google ML Kit: On-device model execution. English-to-Marathi neural translation is performed entirely on your CPU/NPU without cloud transmission.

05. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Mandate)

Under Google Play Developer Policy, all users possess an unconditioned right to request total deletion of their account and all associated telemetry records.

How to Delete Your Data:

  1. In-App Immediate Purge: Open Arriva > Navigate to Settings > Profile > Delete Account. All authentication credentials, local SQLite caches, and review associations will be instantly purged.
  2. Direct Web / Email Request: Send an email to sachinbableshwar1828@gmail.com with the subject line ARRIVA: Account Deletion Request. Specify your registered email address.

Data deletion requests are confirmed and completed within 7 business days. No backups or shadow records are retained.

06. Children's Privacy Protection

Arriva is a general-audience travel utility. We do not intentionally market to or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 18 under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child without verified parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete that information from our servers.
