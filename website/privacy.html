Core Privacy Guarantee & Architecture

ARRIVA operates on a strict Offline-First, Privacy-by-Design architecture. Our applications execute primarily against local sandboxed databases (Room SQLite on Android) and on-device machine learning models (Google ML Kit). We do not assign advertising identifiers, load marketing pixels, or broker behavioral data. We never sell, rent, monetize, or transfer your personal data to commercial data brokers.

Android Runtime Permissions & Disclosures

In compliance with Google Play's Prominent Disclosure and User Consent policies, below are the technical justifications for runtime permissions declared by the ARRIVA Tourist Guide application:

ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Runtime Permission

Technical Purpose: Required to locate your position on topographic hiking trails (osmdroid) and urban transit maps (Google Maps SDK). Coordinates are also used to compute distance vectors in the Simulated Annealing trip planner and to prefill coordinates into your SMS composer during an emergency distress action.
Collection Boundary: Handled in volatile memory strictly while the app is active in the foreground. Never logged to external analytics servers or transmitted without explicit user action. Arriva declares NO background location permission (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION).

CAMERA & READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Optional) Runtime Permission

Technical Purpose: Requested only when you explicitly choose to capture or attach a photo to a regional landmark review.
Collection Boundary: Only triggered on direct user interaction. Arriva never scans, indexes, or accesses photos in the background.

INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Permission

Technical Purpose: Used to download offline map tile packages, authenticate optional accounts via Supabase, and dispatch prompts to our secure backend proxy.
Collection Boundary: Transmitted exclusively over TLS 1.3 encrypted sockets.

EXPLICIT EXCLUSION: ZERO SEND_SMS & CALL_PHONE 100% Intent-Based

To guarantee total Google Play policy compliance and eliminate background eavesdropping risks, ARRIVA does NOT declare SEND_SMS, CALL_PHONE, or BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE. All emergency actions utilize standard Android system Intents (Intent.ACTION_SENDTO and Intent.ACTION_DIAL). You retain full tactile control before any message is sent or call is initiated.

Third-Party Infrastructure Boundary

ARRIVA connects only to essential, vetted infrastructure components required for core utility:

  • Supabase: Manages optional user authentication and community place reviews. Protected by PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS).
  • Render Backend Proxy: Intermediates Claude AI requests and image uploads to prevent client-side secret exposure. Client IP addresses are not stored to disk.
  • Cloudinary: Stores user-submitted place review imagery with automated compression.
  • Google ML Kit: On-device model execution. English-to-Marathi neural translation is performed locally on your device CPU/NPU without cloud transmission.

Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Mandate)

Under Google Play Developer Policy, every user possesses an unconditioned right to request total deletion of their account and all associated telemetry records.

Deletion Pathways:

  1. In-App Immediate Purge: Open Arriva > Settings > Profile > Delete Account. All authentication tokens, local SQLite caches, and review associations will be instantly purged.
  2. Direct Email Request: Send an email to sachinbableshwar1828@gmail.com with the subject line ARRIVA: Account Deletion Request. Specify your registered email address.

Data deletion requests are confirmed and completed within 7 business days. No shadow backups are retained.

Children's Privacy Protection

ARRIVA is a general-audience digital utility. We do not knowingly market to or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 18 under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child without verified parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete that information from our servers.
