01 Negative Scope Manifest

Information We Categorically Do NOT Collect

Arriva adheres to the principle of Radical Minimization. Many consumer travel applications passively scrape user background movements, Wi-Fi beacons, and contact lists to monetize tourism behavior. Arriva deliberately strips out all data brokers:

location_off
No Background Trail Auditing GPS coordinates are not sent to any cloud server when your phone is locked or Arriva is idle.
contact_phone
No Contact Book Ingestion We never request or inspect your phone book, address book, SMS logs, or call logs.
credit_card_off
No Stored Payment Details All ticket bookings forward directly to official Maharashtra State Road Transport (MSRTC) or ASI gates.
fingerprint
No Device Identifiers No collection of IMEI, Android Serial Number, or Advertising IDs for advertising profiling.
02 Hardware Permissions

Android Runtime Permissions and Technological Justifications

When you explore ancient hill forts such as Rajgad, Sinhagad, or Harishchandragad, the application requires minimal hardware hooks to ensure safety and guidance:

my_location
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Foreground Only

Used exclusively during active route guidance, trail topo plotting, and distance reckoning to nearby cisterns and historical bastions. This coordinate never leaves memory.

folder_open
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Scoped Android API 33+

Limited strictly to importing your custom GPX waypoint tracks or saving user-captured expedition logs to your local gallery.

wifi_find
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Passive Query

Determines whether Wi-Fi/Cellular connectivity exists prior to initiating community rockfall alert downloads or vector tile updates, avoiding unnecessary battery drain.

04 Cryptographic Defense

Data Retention, Sandboxing & Security Protocols

Local trail coordinates, personal packing inventories, and annotated maps are stored within an Android EncryptedSharedPreferences wrapper utilizing AES-256 GCM encryption backed by the Android Keystore system.

enhanced_encryption
Transport Layer Security 1.3
All dynamic web communication uses TLS 1.3 encryption with strict public-key pinning to thwart Man-In-The-Middle (MITM) interceptions on public resort Wi-Fi networks.
05 Child Safety & Indian Law

Children's Privacy (COPPA & Information Technology Act Compliance)

Arriva is a general-purpose exploration and heritage documentation suite. We do not knowingly solicit, catalog, or aggregate information from children under the age of 13 in accordance with the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and minors under 18 pursuant to India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023). If you believe a minor has entered cloud-synced information without parental consent, please contact our Grievance Officer below for immediate purge.
