Privacy Notice

ARRIVA ORVIX · Developer Utility

Effective date: November 3, 2026

In development

ORVIX is currently in development and not yet publicly distributed. This notice defines our architectural privacy standards for the tool.

Local Execution Standards

  • On-device processing: All model requests are directed to local endpoints on your machine (e.g., http://127.0.0.1:11434).
  • Zero telemetry: No tracking beacons, analytics scripts, or automated error reporting services are integrated into the application binary.
  • No training usage: Your prompt text, system messages, and model completions are never collected, logged, or shared.
  • Transparent configuration: Network configurations and local API keys remain stored exclusively in local plaintext configurations under your control.

Full Policy Publication

A finalized technical privacy declaration will accompany the initial source release on GitHub.
