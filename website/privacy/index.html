What this website collects

The ARRIVA website is a static informational site built with pure HTML and CSS. We collect no personal data from visitors:

  • No cookies: We do not set or read tracking cookies, session identifiers, or advertising tokens.
  • No analytics trackers: We do not embed Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or third-party behavioral scripts.
  • No third-party CDN requests: All typography (Fraunces, Inter) and icons are self-hosted locally on this domain. Browsing this site triggers zero requests to third-party CDNs.

Hosting infrastructure

This website is hosted on Google Firebase Hosting. As part of standard web server operations, Firebase Hosting may record routine server access logs (such as IP address, user-agent string, and requested URL) for security monitoring and operational stability in accordance with Google's cloud infrastructure policies.

Contact information

If you contact us via email, your email address and message content are used solely to reply to your inquiry. We do not subscribe your email to marketing lists or transfer it to third parties.
