ARRIVA NEXUS.
Zero Feeds. Distraction-Free Messaging.

Modern chat apps are cluttered with social stories, engagement algorithms, and surveillance telemetry. NEXUS is a pure, high-performance messaging client designed around topic channels, direct one-to-one chats, and on-device SQLite message storage.

No Algorithmic Feeds or Stories Your attention belongs to you. Only the messages you sent and received appear.
On-Device SQLite Storage Messages reside securely on your device sandbox with simple self-serve clearing.
Minimal Background Overhead Zero constant analytics pinging. Tested under 2% background battery consumption per day.

ARRIVA ORVIX.
Local AI Engine & Ollama Bridge.

Sending proprietary source code, internal documents, and personal queries to centralized cloud LLMs introduces telemetry risks and recurring token bills. ORVIX bridges your local developer workflow directly to on-device Ollama sockets (127.0.0.1:11434), providing streaming tokens with zero external leaks.

STUDIO FOUNDATIONS

The Four Architectural Principles.

01 · OFFLINE SOVEREIGNTY

Works in Dead Zones

Essential software must work when connectivity is severed. Mountain ridges, underground metros, or rural highways—our core tools never demand an active cloud connection.

02 · ZERO AD SURVEILLANCE

No Brokers. No Trackers.

We do not embed Facebook SDKs, Google Analytics beacons, or third-party behavioral pixels. Your device sandbox is sacred.

03 · LOCAL METAL COMPUTE

On-Device Intelligence

From Google ML Kit translation running on phone NPUs to Ollama LLMs running on your local Apple Silicon or NVIDIA GPU, intelligence runs where you are.

04 · OPEN ARCHITECTURE

Inspectable Source

Our projects are developed in the open on GitHub under the MIT License. Anyone can audit our permission manifests, Gradle builds, and networking code.
