Flagship Spotlight

Tourist Guide for Android

Tourist Guide is our primary focus, preparing for closed testing on Google Play. Built specifically for travelers exploring Maharashtra's historic hill forts, cave temples, Western Ghats trails, and coastal towns.

  • Curated destinations: Heritage sites, viewpoints, and cultural landmarks across Pune, Mumbai, and regional districts.
  • On-device translation: Machine translation between Marathi, Hindi, and English powered locally by Google ML Kit models without data connectivity.
  • Offline contour maps: 20m topographic trail lines rendered with osmdroid for navigation in remote Sahyadri areas lacking cellular reception.
  • Intent-based safety: Emergency assistance triggers standard Android dialer or SMS apps with pre-filled GPS coordinates, keeping you in complete control.

About ARRIVA

ARRIVA is an independent software studio founded and operated by Sachin Bableshwar in Pune, Maharashtra, India. The studio specializes in native Android development, backend architecture, and developer utilities designed for privacy and resilience.

We believe software should solve genuine daily problems, function reliably offline, avoid unnecessary telemetry, and communicate clearly about its architecture and data practices. All public projects are maintained openly on GitHub.
