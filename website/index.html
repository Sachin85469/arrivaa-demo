Engineered Capabilities

Architected for the Rigors of Real Travel.

No cloud dependency when you are traversing remote fort ridges. Arriva runs critical intelligence entirely on your device.

Autonomous Simulated Annealing Planner

Arriva formulates your daily stops as a Travelling Salesperson Problem (TSP) with hard time windows and category diversity penalties. The annealing schedule runs 1,000 cooling cycles on-device in under 40ms, eliminating backtracking and awkward U-turns across Pune traffic.

Algorithmic Optimization Deterministic · Zero Cloud Latency

Offline Marathi Neural Translation

Powered by Google ML Kit's compressed on-device models. Translate signs, menu items, and colloquial directions between English and Marathi without needing a cellular connection.

Dual-Engine Map Architecture

Arriva automatically switches between Google Maps for urban Pune navigation with real-time transit and osmdroid vector tiles for Sahyadri mountain trails, rendering 20-meter elevation contours even in complete dead-zones.

Zero-Permission Emergency Intent SOS

Arriva guarantees 100% Google Play privacy compliance. Unlike apps demanding invasive background SMS permissions, Arriva constructs a precise GPS distress payload and launches Android's native SMS composer and 112 dialer via system Intents.

Frequently Asked Questions.

Arriva downloads OpenStreetMap topographic vector tiles and cultural metadata into a local Room SQLite database while you have Wi-Fi. Google ML Kit's translation model runs entirely on your phone's NPU/CPU without contacting external servers.
Google Play strictly reserves the SEND_SMS permission for default SMS handler apps. To protect your privacy and ensure rapid Play Store approval, Arriva uses standard Android Intents (ACTION_SENDTO and ACTION_DIAL). It pre-populates your exact GPS coordinates into your default messaging app and dials 112 with zero background eavesdropping.
Planning a multi-stop day is an NP-hard Traveling Salesperson Problem. Arriva starts with a random route and iteratively swaps stops, accepting better routes and probabilistic worse routes based on temperature decay. It factors in geographical distance, opening times, typical visit durations, and meal windows.
