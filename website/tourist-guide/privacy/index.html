1. Who We Are

ARRIVA is an independent software studio based in Pune, Maharashtra, India, founded by Sachin Bableshwar. This policy covers the Tourist Guide Android application (package name: com.arriva.touristguideapp).

2. Data We Collect

Account information

If you sign in with email or Google Sign-In, your email address and display name are stored in Supabase. Sign-in is optional.

Location

When you use map features, the app accesses your device's GPS location to show nearby places and calculate routes. Location is used only while the app is in the foreground. The app also requests foreground service location permission to support the SOS emergency feature while it is active.

Audio

If you use voice input for the translator or phrasebook, the app accesses the microphone. Audio is processed on-device and not recorded or transmitted.

Photos and files

Write external storage is requested on Android 12 and below for saving offline map tiles. On Android 13 and above, this permission is not needed.

Chat messages

If you use the AI travel chat, your messages are sent to our backend server (hosted on Render), which forwards them to the Anthropic API. The backend does not store message content to disk.

Place reviews

If you submit a review, the text and any attached photo are stored in Supabase and Cloudinary respectively.

Push notifications

The app requests notification permission on Android 13+ to deliver trip reminders and updates.

We do NOT collect

Advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, contact lists, call logs, or browsing history.

3. Why We Collect This Data

Each piece of data serves a specific app feature: location for maps and routing, email for account recovery and review authorship, audio for translation, photos for reviews, and chat text for AI responses. We do not use collected data for advertising or profiling.

4. Permissions Explained

Permission Purpose When requested
INTERNET Communicate with backend services (Supabase, AI chat proxy, weather data, map tiles) Always granted
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Check whether the device is online before making network requests Always granted
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Show your position on maps, find nearby places, calculate routes, pre-fill GPS coordinates in emergency SMS When you open a map feature
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Approximate positioning fallback When you open a map feature
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below only) Save offline map tile data When you download offline maps
RECORD_AUDIO Voice input for the translator and phrasebook When you tap the voice/microphone button
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Trip reminders and app updates On first relevant action (Android 13+)
FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION Keep the SOS emergency service active while in use When you activate the SOS feature

Note: The app does not declare SEND_SMS, CALL_PHONE, READ_CONTACTS, CAMERA, or ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION. Emergency calling and SMS use Android's standard Intent system, which opens the phone's dialer or SMS app and requires your confirmation before anything is sent.

5. Third-Party Services

Supabase

Hosts user accounts and place data. Supabase's privacy policy applies to data stored on their infrastructure.

Google Maps SDK and Places API

Used for map rendering and place search. Subject to Google's privacy policy.

Google ML Kit

On-device translation between English, Hindi and Marathi. Translation models run locally on your device; no text is sent to Google's servers for this feature.

OpenWeatherMap

Provides weather forecasts for destinations. Your approximate location may be sent to their API.

Anthropic (Claude)

Powers the AI travel chat. Your chat messages are forwarded through our backend proxy hosted on Render. The proxy does not log message content. Anthropic's usage policy applies.

Cloudinary

Stores photos attached to place reviews. Subject to Cloudinary's privacy policy.

NVIDIA API

Used for optional AI-generated travel narratives. Subject to NVIDIA's terms of service.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local data (favourites, cached places, trip plans) is stored on your device in a Room SQLite database. You can clear it at any time through the app settings or by uninstalling.

Account data stored in Supabase can be deleted by going to Settings > Profile > Delete Account within the app. You can also email sachinbableshwar1828@gmail.com with the subject "ARRIVA: Account Deletion Request" and your registered email address. We will process deletion requests within 7 business days.

For a detailed guide on account deletion, see our dedicated account deletion page.

7. Security

We transmit data over encrypted HTTPS connections. API keys for third-party services are not embedded in the app binary; they are stored server-side or in local configuration files excluded from source control. Our backend proxy uses a shared-secret token for request authentication.

8. Children

Tourist Guide is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 18 under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). If we learn that we have collected data from a child without parental consent, we will delete that data.

9. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. If you are in the European Economic Area, you may also have rights under the GDPR. Indian residents may exercise rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
