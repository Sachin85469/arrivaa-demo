Tourist Guide

In testing

A travel companion for exploring Maharashtra — its forts, temples, nature spots and street food.

Join the closed test View source on GitHub

What it does

Tourist Guide helps you discover places across Maharashtra — historical forts, nature reserves, temples, street food spots and shopping areas. Each place includes visitor ratings, opening hours, entry costs, photos and estimated dwell times.

The built-in smart trip planner builds multi-stop day itineraries tailored to who you are travelling with (solo, friends, or family) and your preferred pace. It uses a 16-dimensional taste vector to match places to your interests and runs a Simulated Annealing route optimiser in Kotlin to sequence stops without zigzagging through city traffic.

For communication, the app provides real-time translation between English, Hindi and Marathi using Google ML Kit. Translation runs entirely on your device with no internet connection required. A tourist phrasebook with common categories (transport, dining, emergency) and speech pronunciation is included.

In an emergency, the SOS feature lets you quickly trigger assistance. It launches your phone's dialer pre-filled with 112 or opens your default SMS app with your current GPS coordinates pre-filled. These use standard Android intents, so you always retain full control before a message is sent or call placed.

How it works

The app loads curated destination data from a cloud database hosted on Supabase and caches it in a local Room SQLite database on your device. Once cached, you can browse destinations, view details and access saved itineraries completely offline.

Navigation is supported by two mapping engines: Google Maps SDK for city navigation and places autocomplete, and OpenStreetMap via osmdroid for offline topographic and contour trail maps where cellular coverage is absent.

AI travel chat and itinerary storytelling connect to an Anthropic Claude model through a dedicated Node.js backend proxy hosted on Render. API keys remain protected on the server, and requests from the app authenticate using a shared secret token.

Live destination weather forecasts are retrieved from OpenWeatherMap, and traveler reviews with photos are stored securely using Supabase and Cloudinary.

Under the hood

Tourist Guide is written natively in Kotlin and Java, targeting Android 14 (API level 34) with backward compatibility to Android 7.0 (API level 24).

  • Architecture: AndroidX AppCompat, Material Components, ConstraintLayout, Room SQLite database, Retrofit2, Volley, Ktor client.
  • On-Device ML: Google ML Kit on-device translation for English, Hindi and Marathi.
  • Mapping: Dual engine combining Google Play Services Maps and osmdroid vector tiles.
  • Authentication & Storage: Supabase Auth (email/password and Google Sign-In) and PostgREST client with PostgreSQL Row-Level Security.
  • Licence: Open source under the MIT licence on GitHub.

Privacy and your data

Tourist Guide requests location, microphone and notification permissions only when you use the corresponding feature. The app does not collect advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, or contact lists.
