Account and Data Deletion

Tourist Guide · Android Application

Effective date: November 3, 2026

In compliance with Google Play's user data policies and privacy standards, ARRIVA provides two methods to permanently delete your Tourist Guide account and associated personal data.

Method 1: In-App Deletion

You can delete your account directly inside the Android application:

  1. Open the Tourist Guide application on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to the account settings [confirm in-app navigation: Settings > Profile > Delete Account].
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm your choice when prompted.
  4. Your account profile, authentication record, and associated remote data will be purged immediately from Supabase.

Method 2: Email Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can request manual deletion via email:

  1. Send an email to sachinbableshwar1828@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: ARRIVA: Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the registered email address used for your Tourist Guide account.
  4. We will verify ownership and permanently delete your account and records [confirm deletion turnaround timeframe: typically processed within 7 to 30 business days]. You will receive a confirmation once completed.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Account credentials: Your authentication ID, email address, and profile name stored in Supabase.
  • Submitted reviews: Any destination reviews or ratings you authored.
  • Uploaded images: Any destination photos you uploaded to Cloudinary.

Local Device Data

Data stored locally on your device (bookmarked places, downloaded offline map tiles, local Room SQLite caches) is not stored on our servers. You can clear this local data at any time through Android Settings: Settings > Apps > Tourist Guide > Storage > Clear Storage, or simply by uninstalling the app.

Questions

For assistance with account deletion or data safety inquiries, contact Sachin Bableshwar at sachinbableshwar1828@gmail.com.
