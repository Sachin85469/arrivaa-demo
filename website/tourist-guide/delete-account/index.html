Tourist Guide — Account & Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Under Google Play Developer Policy and ARRIVA's privacy principles, you have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. You can delete your account directly inside the app or by sending an email request.

Option 1: Delete from within the app (Immediate)

If you have the Tourist Guide app installed, you can delete your account instantly through the settings menu:

  1. Open the Tourist Guide app on your device.
  2. Tap the menu or profile icon and navigate to Settings.
  3. Select Profile (or Account Info).
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your decision in the prompt dialog.

Once confirmed, your authentication session is terminated immediately, your profile and account record in Supabase are deleted, and local SQLite cached data is purged from your device.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app or device, you can request manual deletion of your account and data by email:

  1. Send an email to sachinbableshwar1828@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: ARRIVA: Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address registered with your Tourist Guide account so we can verify and locate your records.

We will acknowledge your request and complete the deletion within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once your account and all associated records have been removed.

What data gets deleted

When an account deletion request is processed, the following information is permanently purged:

  • Account credentials: Your Supabase authentication user ID, email address, password hash, and profile display name.
  • User reviews & ratings: Any reviews and ratings you authored for landmarks or places.
  • Uploaded review photos: Any images you uploaded to Cloudinary attached to your reviews.
  • Cloud-synced trips: Custom trip plans and saved itineraries associated with your account.
  • Local cache: When you use in-app deletion or uninstall the app, on-device SQLite database caches (saved places, search history, preferences) are wiped from the device sandbox.

We do not retain shadow backups or sell residual data to third parties.
