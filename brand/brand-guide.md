# ARRIVA Brand Guide

## Brand foundation
**Brand:** ARRIVA  
**Tagline:** Ideas Into Innovation.  
**Role:** Technology brand and community promoting digital products and sharing practical technology, software development, and AI content.

## Personality
- Modern and premium
- Creative and approachable
- Curious and experimental
- Credible and practical

## Voice
Balanced professional and casual. Explain the benefit first, show real product behavior, use simple language, and avoid exaggerated claims.

### Say
- "Here's how it works."
- "Built around a real problem."
- "We're testing, learning, and improving."
- "Explore the product and tell us what you think."

### Avoid
- "Revolutionary" or "world-changing" without evidence
- Fake testimonials, invented metrics, or unsupported comparisons
- Dense jargon when a simple explanation works
- Treating every product as if it serves the same audience

## Visual identity
- **Primary purple:** `#7C3AED`
- **Light lavender:** `#F8F7FF`
- **Deep navy:** `#0F172A`
- **Green accent:** `#10B981`
- **Typeface:** Poppins
- **Logo direction:** bold, geometric, uppercase wordmark only

## Usage
- Let purple be the most recognizable accent.
- Use lavender or white as the main background.
- Use navy for body text and high contrast.
- Use green sparingly for small highlights.
- Keep layouts spacious, readable, and mobile-friendly.
- Prefer genuine app screenshots and screen recordings over generic stock visuals.
- Product brands use the unified naming system: ARRIVA Tourist Guide, ARRIVA NEXUS, ARRIVA ORVIX.

## Product summaries (verify before publishing)
- **ARRIVA Tourist Guide:** a tourist guide app for discovering places and planning experiences.
- **ARRIVA NEXUS:** a chatting app.
- **ARRIVA ORVIX:** a tool that connects to Ollama running locally and exposes an API workflow for development/testing without requiring a paid external API for that local workflow. Do not imply that all models or use cases are free; local hardware and model requirements still apply.
